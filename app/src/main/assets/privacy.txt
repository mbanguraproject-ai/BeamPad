BeamPad Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

BeamPad does not collect your personal data. It does show ads, and this policy explains exactly what that involves.

WHAT BEAMPAD ITSELF COLLECTS

Nothing. BeamPad has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash reporting. Nothing you type, save or send is transmitted to Dev_Bangs, and we cannot see it.

ADVERTISING

On the free plan, BeamPad shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner in Settings, and a full-screen ad that can appear while the app is opening. To serve them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK receives your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and general device information. That data goes to Google, not to Dev_Bangs, and is handled under Google's own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Where the law requires it, BeamPad asks for your consent before requesting any ad, and you can change that choice at any time under Settings, Ad privacy options.

With BeamPad Pro, or the earlier ad removal purchase, the ad SDK stops requesting ads and no ads are shown.

PURCHASES

BeamPad Pro is sold through Google Play as a subscription (and, where offered, a one-time lifetime purchase); earlier versions also sold a one-time ad removal, which keeps working. Payment, free trials, renewal and cancellation are handled entirely by Google Play, and you can cancel a subscription at any time in the Play Store. Dev_Bangs never sees your payment details. BeamPad stores only a local record of what you have bought, which it refreshes from Google Play.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Snippets. Text you save, including wifi passwords and logins, is stored only on your phone. Every snippet value is encrypted using a key generated inside the Android Keystore, which cannot be exported from the device. Snippets are excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer.

Snippets are never sent over the network. They are not shared with the advertising SDK, with Google Play, or with anyone else. They leave your phone only as keystrokes to the device you paired with.

Devices, panels, macros and connection history. The names, kinds, labels and profiles you give your devices, the panels and macros you build, and a short history of recent connection events (used by Diagnostics) are stored only on your phone. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, and are never sent over the network. The connection history leaves your phone only if you choose to share a diagnostics report, and that report never includes anything you typed.

Settings. Your preferences, such as keyboard layout and appearance, are stored locally and may be included in your Android backup.

PERMISSIONS AND WHY THEY ARE NEEDED

Internet and network state. Required by the advertising SDK and by Google Play billing. BeamPad's own features do not use the network.

Advertising ID. Used by the advertising SDK to serve and measure ads.

Bluetooth (connect and advertise). Required for the phone to present itself as a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse to your TV. BeamPad does not collect information about nearby devices beyond the one you choose to pair with.

Notifications. Used only for the ongoing notification that keeps the keyboard connection alive.

Biometric or device credential. Used only to confirm it is you before a protected snippet is sent. BeamPad never receives your fingerprint or PIN; the Android system performs the check and reports only success or failure.

WHAT IS SENT TO YOUR TV

When you send text or move the pointer, that input goes over Bluetooth to the device you paired with, exactly as a physical keyboard or mouse would. It is not stored by BeamPad.

CHILDREN

BeamPad is not directed at children.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app.
